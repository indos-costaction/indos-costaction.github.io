Slides, notebooks and hands-on guides from the [Working Group 3 Training School](/training),
held in Madrid and online from 30 September to 2 October 2026. Each presenter keeps their
materials in a public repository under the
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) licence, and every release of a
repository is archived on [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org) with a DOI, so the materials can be
reused and cited.

Sessions marked *Materials to come* are linked here as soon as their presenters share them.

## Repositories

To cite a set of materials, use the DOI of its repository. Until a repository has its first
release, cite its GitHub address.
